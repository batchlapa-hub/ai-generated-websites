Curriculum & Study Plans

Explore the structured academic path for Electrical Engineering and Computing. This program is divided into distinct phases, starting with fundamental sciences and advancing to specialized electives in computing and power management.

Course Structure Overview

The curriculum is organized into four major phases. The first year covers mathematics, physics, and introductory programming. Subsequent years introduce core electrical engineering concepts such as circuit analysis and electromagnetism. Advanced modules focus on the integration of computing technologies with hardware systems.

Workload Distribution by Phase

Students progress from foundational coursework to specialized research projects. The final phase includes a mandatory internship and thesis work, ensuring practical experience before graduation. Progression through these stages is tracked via the ECTS credit system.

Admissions & Program Requirements

What are the language requirements for admission?

Instruction is conducted in Portuguese. Applicants must demonstrate proficiency equivalent to B2 level or possess prior certification from the institution.

Is an internship mandatory for graduation?

Yes, a professional internship totaling 6 months is required as part of the final phase of the degree program.

Can I choose between power systems or computing tracks?

In later years, students may select elective courses that align with their interest in either electrical grids or advanced computer science applications.
