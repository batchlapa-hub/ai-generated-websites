AKC & FCI Breed Standard

Official requirements for structure, coat, and temperament.

Physical Attributes Checklist

  • Coat Texture

    Short, dense, and harsh on the outside with a soft undercoat for water resistance.

  • The 'Otter' Tail

    Thick at the base and tapering to a point; must be docked in working lines or natural length in show lines depending on registration.

  • Eye Color Requirements

    Dark brown is preferred, though light amber or hazel may appear but can disqualify a dog from conformation shows.

Lineage Comparison: Show vs. Working

  • Show Line Yellow Lab

    Broader skull and heavier bone structure prioritizing appearance.

  • Working Line Black Lab

    Sleeker head shape and leaner muscle mass optimized for retrieving.

Locating Standard-Compliant Breeders

  • Health Tested Breeder Search

    Use the AKC database to find breeders who perform OFA and PRA tests.

  • Field Trial Registration

    Connect with organizations like NADAC for working line puppies.

Verify Health Clearances

Never buy a puppy without seeing the OFA hip and elbow dysplasia numbers from both parents.
