Characteristics & Roles

Beyond their reputation as friendly family pets, Labradors are highly specialized working dogs. Their physical structure is optimized for endurance and strength, while their cognitive abilities allow them to master complex tasks ranging from guide work to advanced field trials.

"The Labrador is defined by a coat that withstands the elements and a temperament that welcomes the stranger."

**Coat & Colors:** The breed standard recognizes three colors: Black, Yellow (ranging from fox-red to silver), and Chocolate. Each color variant carries distinct genetic markers but shares the same dense, water-resistant double coat designed for protection against cold.

**Temperament:** Characterized by friendliness and high intelligence, Labradors are eager to please. This trait makes them natural candidates for service work, where they must remain calm in chaotic environments while maintaining an open demeanor toward people of all backgrounds.

What is the primary exercise requirement for a Labrador?

Labradors need approximately one hour of vigorous activity daily. This prevents boredom behaviors and supports their high energy levels.

Are chocolate-colored Labradors genetically different from black ones?

Yes, the chocolate gene is recessive to both yellow and black genes, making it a distinct genetic expression within the breed.
