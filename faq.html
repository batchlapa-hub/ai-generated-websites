Frequently Asked Questions

How long does standard shipping take?

Orders placed before 2 PM EST ship the same day. Standard delivery typically arrives within 3-5 business days depending on your location.

Are your products tested on animals?

No. All MAKEUP products are cruelty-free and never tested on animals. We also do not sell to markets that require animal testing.

What is your return policy for unopened items?

We accept returns on all unopened, unused products within 30 days of delivery. Please keep the original packaging intact and include your order number.
