FAQ: Common Questions About Bondage & Safety

How do I get started with bondage?

Start by learning the basics of consent, communication, and safety. Our 'Bondage Basics' section is a great place to begin.

What are some essential safety tips for beginners?

Always use a safe word, check circulation regularly, and never tie someone without their full consent.

How do I care for my rope equipment?

Clean your ropes with mild soap and water. Store them in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight to maintain quality.
