Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer aquarium setup services?

Yes! We provide both basic and advanced aquarium setup packages tailored to your specific needs.

Can I return a fish if it doesn't survive in my tank?

We stand by our products. If a fish does not thrive within 14 days of purchase, we will replace it at no cost.

How often should I clean my aquarium?

Regular maintenance is key. We recommend partial water changes every two weeks and cleaning filters monthly.
