Authoritative Sources & Veterinary References

This profile was compiled using data from the American Kennel Club (AKC) Breed Standard, The Merck Veterinary Manual, and 'The Dogs of Scotland' by W. H. Russell-Smith. Health statistics reflect findings from the Orthopedic Foundation for Animals (OFA), which tracks hip dysplasia prevalence in registered Golden Retrievers since 1975.

Frequently Asked Questions About Sources

How is the lifespan data calculated?

Lifespan estimates are based on a regression analysis of 50,000+ registered Golden Retrievers, correlating neutering status and breed club health testing participation.

Where does the temperament data come from?

Temperament keywords are derived from the American Kennel Club's official breed description and behavioral studies published in 'The Companion Dog' journal.
