Methodology & Source Compilation

The data presented in this editorial compilation is derived from peer-reviewed veterinary studies and historical breed club records. We prioritize sources that offer longitudinal health tracking rather than anecdotal reports.

Key reference points include the Orthopedic Foundation for Animals (OFA) database, which provides standardized hip dysplasia scoring, and the American Kennel Club's breed standards established in 1903. Historical accounts regarding the breed's development at Lord Tweedmouth’s estate are cross-referenced with genetic lineage registries to ensure accuracy.
