The Collection: Motion Mechanics

This deep dive explores the specific engineering constraints and advantages of our three primary window types. Each mechanism is designed to function within our proprietary 18mm frame depth.

Series Specifications and Applications

The Pivot series utilizes a concealed pivot bearing that allows the sash to rotate up to 360 degrees. This is ideal for creating operable ventilation in spaces where sliding rails are undesirable, such as over fireplaces or beneath low-hanging ceilings.

Our Curve collection requires specialized bending jigs during fabrication to achieve radii as tight as R250 without compromising the structural bond of the aluminum alloy. These units often feature custom-finished powder coatings that match specific architectural palettes requested by the client.

The Vertical Sliding system employs a dual-catch mechanism for enhanced security and wind-load resistance up to 180 mph. The tracks are manufactured from high-grade stainless steel, hidden entirely within the rebate of the frame profile.

Installation and Maintenance

What is the minimum wall thickness required for installation?

Our slim profiles fit into standard 4-inch masonry reveals, though a minimum of 3.5 inches allows for full thermal break integration.

How often do the magnetic catches require lubrication?

The self-lubricating polymer pads are pre-treated and designed to last the lifetime of the product without maintenance.

Can the Curve series be installed on existing rectangular openings?

Yes, but the opening requires reinforcement at the corner points where the bending occurs. We provide a structural retrofit kit for this purpose.
