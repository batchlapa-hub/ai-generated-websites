Research & Innovation Centers

The department fosters innovation through dedicated research centers addressing national energy needs. Our faculty engages in projects ranging from smart grid optimization to embedded computing for industrial automation.

Current Research Focus Areas

Active laboratories investigate the stability of renewable power networks and the development of low-power computing architectures. Researchers collaborate with industry partners to test new technologies in real-world environments, ensuring academic output meets professional standards.
