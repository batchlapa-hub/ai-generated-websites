Upcoming Events & Festivities

Our calendar is filled with celebrations that blend ancient traditions with modern artistic expression. Whether you are a local resident or a visitor, these events define the social rhythm of Condeixa-a-Nova.

The Feast of Santa Cristina: September Celebration

The annual celebration of Santa Cristina marks one of our most significant community gatherings. The festivities begin with the blessing of the village flag at dawn, followed by the traditional procession carrying the statue through the historic streets where 19th-century balconies still line the route. The weekend includes a culinary competition featuring local pork products and regional wines, alongside workshops on traditional pottery techniques held in the municipal cultural center.

Visitor Guidelines & Event Details

Are there parking restrictions during the Santa Cristina festival?

Yes, temporary parking zones are established on the municipal grounds. Visitors arriving after 09:00 should use the designated overflow lots at the regional highway exit.

Is guided tour access available for the heritage sites during events?

Guided tours run every hour starting at 11:00 AM, led by certified archaeology students from the University of Coimbra. Reservations are recommended via our tourism desk.

Where can I find accessibility information for the event venues?

Wheelchair-accessible routes are marked on all festival maps available at the main plaza and online on our portal homepage.
