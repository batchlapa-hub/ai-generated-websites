Frequently Asked Questions

What is the return policy for opened skincare?

We accept returns on unopened items within 30 days. Once a pump or jar is opened, we cannot restock due to hygiene regulations.

Are your products safe for sensitive skin?

Yes, all formulas are non-comedogenic and tested on reactive skin types. We exclude common allergens like parabens and synthetic fragrances.

How long does shipping take to Europe?

Standard delivery takes 5-7 business days via DHL Express. Expedited options are available at checkout for 2-day arrival.
