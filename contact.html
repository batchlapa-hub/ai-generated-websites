Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

We offer standard and expedited shipping options. Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days, while expedited is available for an additional charge.

Can I return a bottle if I'm not satisfied?

Yes! We accept returns within 30 days of purchase with the original receipt and unopened bottle. Please contact us first for instructions.

Do you offer gift wrapping or special packaging?

Absolutely! Choose a gift option during checkout, and we'll wrap your selection in elegant paper and include a personalized note at no extra cost.
