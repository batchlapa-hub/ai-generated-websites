Licenciatura em Engenharia Eletrotécnica e de Computadores

Prepare for a career at the intersection of power systems and digital innovation through our accredited engineering program. The Faculty of Engineering offers a rigorous curriculum designed to produce graduates capable of solving complex challenges in renewable energy, automation, and computer science.

Program Objectives & Accreditation

The degree is fully accredited by the Portuguese Association for Quality Assurance in Higher Education (A3ES). Our educational model combines theoretical foundations with practical application, ensuring graduates are ready to enter the workforce or pursue advanced research. We focus on developing critical thinking and technical proficiency essential for modern engineering roles.

Program Specifications

  • Accreditation Status Fully Accredited by A3ES
  • Duration of Study 4 Years / 240 ECTS
  • Language of Instruction Portuguese (PT)
  • Core Competencies Power Systems, Computing Integration, Sustainability
