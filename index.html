The Home of Rally: History on Display

Hosted annually since 1986, the Vodafone Rally de Portugal is one of the oldest and most prestigious events in the World Rally Championship (WRC). Known for its dramatic mountain passes and volcanic landscapes, it challenges drivers with high-speed gravel sectors that demand absolute precision. The event traverses the rugged north coast before descending into the historic south, offering a unique blend of natural beauty and technical difficulty.
