The Golden Retriever

Originally developed as gun dogs in the Scottish Highlands, this breed combines a gentle soul with remarkable intelligence. Their distinctive double coat and friendly disposition have made them one of the most beloved companions for families worldwide.

Origins & Physical Traits

Developed in the 19th century by Lord Tweedmouth, these dogs were bred to retrieve waterfowl and upland game. Their athletic build features a deep chest and strong hindquarters designed for endurance. The coat consists of a dense undercoat and longer outer hair that can be cream, golden, or light red.
