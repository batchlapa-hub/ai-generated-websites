Early Life and Influences

Born in the Pacific Northwest, early exposure to local scene dynamics fostered a distinct musical philosophy. Formative years were spent analyzing the interplay between lyrical narrative and sonic texture. The artist frequently cites late 1970s post-punk movements as foundational training grounds. A rigorous approach to songwriting was adopted during high school composition classes at Central High School, where minimalist arrangements were prioritized over technical complexity.

“The goal is not to fill the silence with noise, but to make the silence speak for itself through careful arrangement.”
Excerpt from 2018 Interview Archive
