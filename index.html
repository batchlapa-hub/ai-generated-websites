Municipal Bulletin: Restoration of the Roman Forum Begins

Archaeologists have commenced the Phase II excavation of the Conimbriga ruins, focusing on the newly discovered thermal bath complex. This 180-year-old site will remain open to visitors via a new glass-walled walkway starting next month. The project aims to stabilize the aqueduct walls and integrate modern lighting that highlights the original Roman masonry without altering its texture.

Community Calendar: Celebrating Santa Cristina

Mark your calendars for September, when our town square transforms for the annual Feast of Santa Cristina. The three-day festival includes a traditional folk procession starting at 10:00 AM on Saturday, culminating in the fireworks display over the river valley at midnight. Visitors are encouraged to explore the local artisan stalls set up along the promenade and attend the live string quartet performances scheduled for the main plaza.
