Core Characteristics

Product Series

Our catalog focuses on three distinct motion mechanisms, each engineered to maximize glass-to-frame ratio while maintaining structural integrity.

The Pivot series operates around a central axis, allowing the sash to rotate for precise ventilation control without obstructing sightlines. This mechanism is particularly suited for floor-to-ceiling glazing where traditional handles would interrupt the vertical flow of the facade.

Our Curve collection introduces geometric fluidity into rigid structures, utilizing specialized bending technology on aluminum alloys to create seamless 90-degree corners that appear continuous from a distance. These units are currently deployed in high-end residential projects requiring curved facades.

Finally, the Vertical Sliding system offers effortless operation via concealed magnetic catches and friction rollers. Unlike standard sliders, our track is embedded within the frame profile itself, eliminating protruding hardware and maintaining an uninterrupted vertical plane.
