Labrador Retriever

The Labrador Retriever stands as one of the most adaptable and beloved breeds in modern history. Originally developed for retrieving game on English estates, their gentle disposition combined with high intelligence has made them indispensable companions, service partners, and working dogs across the globe.

Renowned for an affectionate nature, Labradors thrive in households that can provide consistent mental stimulation. Their water-resistant double coat allows them to excel in aquatic environments, while their steady temperament makes them ideal candidates for police and search-and-rescue operations. Whether working a field trial or relaxing beside a family on the sofa, the breed maintains a balance of playfulness and maturity.

  • Origin Newfoundland / Canada
  • Height 21.5–24.5 inches
  • Weight 55–80 lbs
  • Lifespan 10–12 years

Breed Standard Consensus

  • Rin Tin Tin (1927)

    The legendary star of silent films, Rin Tin Tin was rescued as a puppy and became a cultural icon for the breed.

  • Lassie (1954–1960)

    Perhaps the most famous fictional dog in history, Lassie solidified the image of the loyal retriever companion.
