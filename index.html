Golden Retriever

Originating from the Scottish Highlands in the late nineteenth century, this breed was developed by Lord Tweedmouth to provide a loyal companion for hunting and field work. Their distinctive golden coat and friendly demeanor have made them one of the most popular family pets worldwide.

History and Origin

The Golden Retriever traces its roots to Scotland, where Lord Tweedmouth began breeding dogs in 1868. He sought a gundog that could retrieve game without damaging the birds or fish. Through selective breeding of a yellow coat St. John's water spaniel with various hunting breeds, he established the foundation for this gentle, intelligent breed.

Quick Stats

  • Weight Range 51 to 70 pounds
  • Height at Shoulder 21.5 to 24 inches
  • Life Expectancy 10 to 12 years

Appearance and Coat

These dogs are medium-sized with a sturdy, athletic build. Their double coat is dense and water-repellent, ranging in color from light cream to dark golden. The coat requires regular maintenance but sheds seasonally, necessitating consistent brushing to prevent matting around the ears and legs.
