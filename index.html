Biblioteca Joanina: The Baroque Library of King John V

Founded in 1728 by King John V, this magnificent library stands as a testament to the Portuguese Golden Age. As the oldest surviving library in Portugal and a UNESCO World Heritage site, its interior dome is a masterpiece of Baroque artistry. Visitors can explore the unique ecosystem of over 40 bat species that inhabit the cupola, coexisting peacefully with centuries-old manuscripts.
