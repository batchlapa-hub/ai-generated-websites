The Evolving Bookstore

Founded in 2018 by former rare book dealer Sarah Jenkins, Literary Discovery Hub began as a digital archive for obscure press titles. Today, we bridge the gap between dusty archives and modern readers through our curated selection of over 45,000 titles. Our team of three senior curators personally inspects every shipment to ensure high-quality editions and accurate descriptions.
