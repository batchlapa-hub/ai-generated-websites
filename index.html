Golden Retriever

The Golden Retriever is a medium-to-large gun dog bred for retrieving game and assisting hunters in the field. Originally developed in Scotland, this breed was specifically designed to carry waterfowl from deep water and rough terrain back to the hunter without damaging the bird's feathers or skin.

Quick Facts

  • Height (Male) 23–24 inches
  • Height (Female) 21.5–22.5 inches
  • Weight 60–75 lbs
  • Lifespan 10–12 years
  • Group Sporting Group
  • Energy Level High

#appearance

The Golden Retriever features a straight, well-angulated top line that leads into a strong tail. Their coat is dense and of medium length on the body, with longer feathering on the back of the forelegs, breeches covering the hocks, and flowing plumes on the back of the stifle (back of the knee). The head is large and well-proportioned with a broad skull that tapers to a moderately long muzzle. Eyes are dark brown and set obliquely, while ears are medium-sized, set high on the side of the head, and drop close against the skull.

The coat color ranges from light golden to dark golden, never red or mahogany. While puppies often have lighter coats that darken with age, some individuals may lighten slightly as they mature. The tail is thick at the base and tapers to a fine point, carried low when moving but raised in greeting.
