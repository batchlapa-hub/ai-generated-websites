Hiking: Definition & Overview

In the context of outdoor recreation, hiking is defined as a long, vigorous walk, usually on trails or footpaths in nature. Unlike walking, which occurs at any pace and elevation change, hiking implies sustained travel over uneven terrain for distances exceeding three miles (5 km) or lasting more than two hours. This manual serves as an authoritative reference guide to the sport's history, necessary equipment, safety protocols, and primary destinations worldwide.

Essential Metrics

  • Average Daily Distance 8–12 miles (13–19 km)
  • Standard Pack Weight 20% of total body weight
  • Primary Elevation Gain Up to 4,000 ft per day
