Monte Carlo Rally: Snow-Covered Roads and Frozen Tires

Since its inception in 1911, the Monte Carlo Rally has stood as the crown jewel of the World Rally Championship. Drivers face treacherous ice-slicked tarmac and freezing temperatures that test every mechanical component before the first gear is even changed.

The Evolution of the Sport

During the Group B era in the 1980s, cars became increasingly powerful and unstable. Manufacturers pushed boundaries without sufficient safety regulations, leading to a series of catastrophic accidents that ultimately ended the class.
