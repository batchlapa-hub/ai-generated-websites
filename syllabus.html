Deep Dive: Temperature Settings Explained

Temperature is a hyperparameter that controls the randomness of predictions during token generation. A temperature setting of 0.0 forces the model to select the most probable next token at every step, resulting in deterministic and repetitive outputs ideal for coding tasks or data extraction. Conversely, a high temperature (e.g., 1.5) flattens the probability distribution, allowing less likely tokens to be selected, which encourages creativity but introduces hallucinations. The sweet spot varies by task; creative writing usually benefits from higher temperatures, while logical reasoning requires lower settings to maintain chain-of-thought integrity.
