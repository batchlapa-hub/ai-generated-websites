Constitutional AI Framework

We developed the Constitutional AI framework to ensure our models remain helpful, harmless, and honest. This approach moves beyond simple rule-based filters to instill a principled understanding of safety constraints within the model itself.

Core Principles in Action

The Constitution outlines three fundamental pillars that govern every response our AI generates.

Harmlessness requires the system to refuse requests that could lead to physical or psychological damage. Helpfulness ensures that answers are relevant and actionable, even when dealing with sensitive topics. Honesty mandates that the model acknowledges its knowledge cutoffs and avoids presenting speculation as fact.

This iterative refinement process allows us to continuously update our guidelines based on real-world feedback from developers and end users alike.
