Structural Classification of Breeds

Breeds are categorized based on their historical function and physical adaptations. Working dogs are subdivided into retrievers, sporting types, and scenthounds, reflecting their original utility to humans. Herding breeds display distinct head shapes and coat lengths adapted for managing livestock in open fields.

The directory utilizes standards established by major kennel clubs to ensure consistent classification. Entries include references to the American Kennel Club (AKC), the Fédération Cynologique Internationale (FCI), and national registries where applicable.

Common Questions on Breed Identification

How is a breed officially recognized?

Recognition typically requires adherence to specific physical standards and temperament traits defined by national kennel clubs like the AKC or FCI.

What distinguishes mixed breeds from purebreds?

Purebred dogs have documented pedigrees tracing back several generations to a recognized breed standard, whereas mixed breeds lack this verified lineage.
