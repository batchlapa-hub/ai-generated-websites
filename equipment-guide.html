Equipment & Rope Guide

Rope Materials

Learn about the differences between cotton, hemp, and synthetic ropes. Cotton is best for beginners due to its softness, while hemp offers more durability.

Storage & Cleaning Tips

Store your rope in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Clean regularly with mild soap and water to maintain hygiene and longevity.

Beginner Recommendations

We recommend starting with 6mm braided cotton ropes and a pair of safety scissors for emergency releases.
