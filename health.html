Health Considerations & Genetics

Understanding hereditary conditions to ensure a long, healthy life.

Common Genetic Conditions

  • Hip Dysplasia (HD)

    Malformation of the hip joint; screened via X-ray through OFA.

  • Elbow Dysplasia

    Abnormal development of elbow structures causing arthritis pain.

  • Subvalvular Aortic Stenosis (SVAS)

    Heart defect where the aortic valve is obstructed; requires cardiac screening.

  • 1 in 50 Estimated SVAS Incidence
  • 20-30% Hip Scored as 'Fair' or 'Poor'
  • < 4 Weeks Average Onset of Obesity Issues

Owner Health Questions

Should I test my Labrador for SVAS?

Yes. Subvalvular Aortic Stenosis is a common issue in Labradors and should be tested via cardiac auscultation or ultrasound before breeding to avoid passing it on.

How do I check for eye issues like PRA?

Ask your breeder if the parents have been tested by the Canine Eye Registration Foundation (CERF) or OFA to rule out Progressive Retinal Atrophy.
