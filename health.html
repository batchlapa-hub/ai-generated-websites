Our 50-year research legacy focuses on identifying and eliminating genetic defects before they reach the population.

  • 15% Reduction in PRA markers since 2020 screening began

  • 600+ Tests Annual DNA screenings processed by club partners

Our History of Longevity

Established in 1974, the Labrador Club of America pioneered health testing for dogs before it became an industry standard. We maintain a comprehensive database tracking hip and elbow scores alongside genetic markers to ensure breeders make informed decisions that prioritize longevity over appearance.

Breed Health Questions

How are DNA test results verified?

All submissions undergo independent review by our veterinary pathology board before entering the public database.

Can I submit my own dog's health data?

Yes, members can upload hip/elbow evaluation reports from OFA or PennHIP to help build the collective dataset.

What breeds are eligible for club testing programs?

Our program currently focuses on purebred Labrador Retrievers registered with major kennel clubs, though we are expanding criteria annually.
