Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set up Apple TV with my home network?

Connect your Apple TV to your Wi-Fi router and follow the on-screen instructions. Ensure your router supports 5GHz for optimal performance.

Can I use Apple TV with an older iPhone model?

Yes, Apple TV is compatible with all iPhones running iOS 13 or later. Check your device settings to confirm compatibility.

What should I do if my Apple TV isn’t responding?

Try restarting your Apple TV by holding the power button for 10 seconds. If the issue persists, contact support or use live chat.
