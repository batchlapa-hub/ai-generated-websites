Architecture Overview

Our model family is structured to balance raw intelligence with safety alignment. Each tier—Opus, Sonnet, and Haiku—is optimized for different throughput needs while maintaining strict adherence to our Constitutional AI framework.

Reasoning capabilities have expanded significantly in recent versions, allowing the system to handle multi-step logic chains without losing context or accuracy. We maintain a high bar for honesty, ensuring that when information is uncertain, the model explicitly states its limitations rather than hallucinating facts.
