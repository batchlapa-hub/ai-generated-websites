Origins of the St. John's Water Dog

From Newfoundland fisherman’s assistant to American sporting icon.

Evolution From The Cod Fisheries

The breed traces its roots back to St. John's, Newfoundland, in the 18th century. Fishermen needed a dog that could swim strong enough to reach nets and retrieve lost gear from the churning sea. Crossbreeding with local Newfoundland dogs created a smaller, faster, but equally powerful retriever.

  • Early 20th Century US Popularity

    American sportsmen adopted them for hunting and dog shows.

Naming Confusions

Why are they called 'Labradors'?

They were named after the Labrador Peninsula in Canada, where fishermen from St. John's settled and bred them. This distinguishes them from the larger Newfoundland dogs that share similar origins.
