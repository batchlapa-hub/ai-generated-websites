A Legacy of Excellence in Winemaking

For over a century, VINUM has been dedicated to crafting wines that reflect the soul of the vineyard.

Our Heritage and Philosophy

VINUM was founded in 1923 by Enzo Rossi, a passionate winemaker who believed that great wine comes from the land. Today, we continue his legacy with a commitment to sustainability, quality, and tradition.
