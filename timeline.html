Milestones in Rally History

  1. 1973

    First Official WRC Season

    The World Rally Championship was officially recognized by the FIA.

  2. 2017

    Hybrid Era Begins

    Toyota introduces hybrid technology to rally cars, changing power delivery dynamics forever.

  3. 2025

    Current Hybrid Dominance

    The balance between electric motor assistance and traditional combustion engines is finely tuned.

Era Highlights

  • Group B Era 1982-1986: High-speed, unstable supercars.
  • Super 1600 Class 1997-2004: Affordable cars for privateers.

Technological Shifts

The transition from Group A to the modern Rally1 regulations marked a significant shift in performance and safety. The introduction of hybrid powertrains has allowed for more consistent lap times across diverse terrain.
