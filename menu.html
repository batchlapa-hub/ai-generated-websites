The Chef's Philosophy

Executive Chef Maria Santos believes that fusion isn't about mixing ingredients randomly; it is about finding the harmonious balance between cultures. Her kitchen in Los Angeles sources fish from Tokyo and spices from Lima, preparing them with a Brazilian soul to create dishes that tell a story on every plate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your ingredients sustainable?

Yes, we partner with certified sustainable fisheries in Japan and Peru to ensure our ocean is protected.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Our kitchen can prepare gluten-free, vegan, and nut-free versions of most dishes. Please ask your server before ordering.

What are the spice levels like?

We offer a spectrum from mild to 'ají' hot. Our Peruvian-inspired rolls can be customized to your heat tolerance.
